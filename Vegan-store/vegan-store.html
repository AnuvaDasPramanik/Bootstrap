Step 01. Reduced Cancer Risk

A vegan diet, focusing on plant-based foods, offers numerous health benefits, including reduced risk of heart disease, weight management, and improved blood sugar control. It also provides a variety of essential nutrients and may decrease the risk of certain cancers.

Step 02. Heart Health

Vegans tend to have lower LDL ("bad") cholesterol and blood pressure, which reduces the risk of heart disease. The focus on plant-based fats from nuts and other vegetables, rather than animal products , can significantly lower the risk of heart attack

Step 04. Blood Sugar Control

Vegans tend to have lower blood sugar levels and higher insulin sensitivity, potentially reducing the risk of type 2 diabetes. A vegan diet can help improve insulin sensitivity, making it easier for the body to regulate blood sugar..
